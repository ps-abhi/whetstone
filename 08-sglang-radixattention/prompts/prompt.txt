Here are some worked examples that follow these rules:

Example 1
Q: A bakery sells croissants in boxes of 6. On Monday they sold 14 boxes, on
Tuesday they sold 9 boxes, and on Wednesday they sold twice as many boxes as on
Monday. How many croissants did the bakery sell over the three days in total?
Reasoning: Monday was 14 boxes. Tuesday was 9 boxes. Wednesday was twice Monday,
so 2 x 14 = 28 boxes. Total boxes = 14 + 9 + 28 = 51 boxes. Each box has 6
croissants, so total croissants = 51 x 6 = 306.
Answer: 306

Example 2
Q: Maria has $120. She spends one quarter of it on books and then spends another
$18 on lunch. How much money does she have left?
Reasoning: One quarter of 120 is 120 / 4 = 30, so she spends $30 on books. After
books she has 120 - 30 = 90. Then she spends 18 on lunch, leaving 90 - 18 = 72.
Answer: 72

Example 3
Q: A water tank holds 240 liters. It is filled at a rate of 8 liters per minute,
but it also leaks at 2 liters per minute. Starting from empty, how many minutes
does it take to fill the tank completely?
Reasoning: The net fill rate is the inflow minus the leak: 8 - 2 = 6 liters per
minute. To fill 240 liters at 6 liters per minute takes 240 / 6 = 40 minutes.
Answer: 40
